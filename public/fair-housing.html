Equal Housing Opportunity Statement

Blue Rain Holdings LLC and its related companies and subsidiaries are fully committed to compliance with the Federal Fair Housing Act, the Texas Fair Housing Act, and all local municipal ordinances.

We proudly provide equal housing opportunities to all individuals. We do not discriminate against any person because of:

  • Race or Color
  • National Origin or Ancestry
  • Religion
  • Sex (including Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity)
  • Familial Status (having children under 18 or being pregnant)
  • Handicap / Disability
  • Age
  • Veteran Status

We welcome and accommodate reasonable modifications and accommodations for individuals with disabilities, including the lawful use of assistance, service, or emotional support animals.
